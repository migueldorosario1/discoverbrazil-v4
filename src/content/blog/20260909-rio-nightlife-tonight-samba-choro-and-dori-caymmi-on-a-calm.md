Tuesday, 18 August 2026, is a calm midweek in Rio de Janeiro — no stadium show, and Monday's street samba at Pedra do Sal is done for the week. But the city's nightlife is far from quiet, with samba, choro, a free classical concert and a songbook legend all on the menu.

According to riotimesonline.com, the night's headline is Dori Caymmi at Teatro Municipal Ipanema, while Lapa's most dependable samba room, Carioca da Gema, hosts Três a Varejo. Copacabana's tiny Bip Bip runs its Tuesday choro roda, and the UFRJ symphony plays a free concert in a Centro church.

## The Headline: Dori Caymmi in Ipanema

The great songwriter plays Teatro Municipal Ipanema at 8 pm, one of four Tuesdays this month. Tickets are R$80 (US$24), or R$40 (US$12) at the half-price rate. The small municipal theatre, a block from the beach, is a fitting setting for music that defines much of the Brazilian songbook.

## The Safe Bet: Samba de Raiz at Carioca da Gema

Carioca da Gema, Lapa's most awarded samba house since 2000, opens its doors at 7:30 pm with the main set at 8:30 pm. Tonight, Três a Varejo plays samba de raiz in an intimate, air-conditioned room with good food and drinks. The cover is R$30 (US$6) from Tuesday to Thursday. Reserved tables are held only until 9 pm — after that, it's first-come.

## Free and Beautiful: UFRJ Symphony in Centro

The Orquestra Sinfônica da UFRJ plays the Igreja de São Francisco de Paula on Largo de São Francisco at 6 pm, and it costs nothing. The eighteenth-century church is a ten-minute walk from Lapa, and the early start makes it perfect before dinner.

## Local and Legendary: Choro at Bip Bip

Copacabana's Bip Bip, a hole-in-the-wall legend, hosts its Tuesday choro roda from about 8 pm. No cover — you pay for drinks on an honesty-style tab, so bring cash in small notes. Get there early for a plastic chair on the pavement.

## More Options Tonight

Choro Batucada plays Glorioso Cultural in Catete at 7 pm, with tickets about R$30 (US$6) in advance and R$50 (US$10) on the door. Isabella Taviani performs at Teatro João Caetano in Centro at 6:30 pm, with tickets from a couple of reais — one of the cheapest nights out downtown.

For a loose, late alternative, Vaca Atolada on the Lapa edge runs Tuesday to Saturday, 4 pm to 4 am. It's more bar than concert hall — come for the atmosphere, not a specific band.

## The Circuit: When to Go Where

Start early at 6 pm with the free UFRJ symphony concert in Centro, or a beer at the kiosks along Avenida Mem de Sá under the Arcos. The main event at 8 pm is Dori Caymmi in Ipanema or Três a Varejo at Carioca da Gema from 8:30 pm — pick one; they are half an hour apart by car.

For late-night choro, head to Bip Bip from 8 pm. When it's time to go home, book a car rather than hunting for a late bus, and wait in a lit, busy spot.

## Culture and Clubs

CCBB Rio is closed on Tuesdays, but the Museu do Amanhã and MAR in the port are both open — MAR is free on Tuesdays. Rio Scenarium and the Lapa clubs are weekend-led, mostly Wednesday/Thursday to Saturday, so save them for later in the week.

- Free and beautiful: the UFRJ symphony at Igreja de São Francisco de Paula, 6 pm.

Tuesday in Rio is a night for the connoisseur — intimate rooms, real music and a local pace. Choose your corner and enjoy.
