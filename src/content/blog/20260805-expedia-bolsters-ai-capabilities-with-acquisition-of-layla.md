Expedia Group has taken a significant step to bolster its AI-powered services with the acquisition of Layla, a Berlin-based AI trip-planning and booking startup. The deal was announced on Friday, with Expedia aiming to accelerate the deployment of its specialized AI agents through this strategic move. While the financial terms were not disclosed, the acquisition highlights Expedia's commitment to incorporating advanced technology into its travel services.

Founded in 2023, Layla has managed to secure a total funding of 5 million euros ($5.7 million) from investors such as First Minute Capital and M13, as reported by Crunchbase. Layla's platform is designed to assist travelers by utilizing conversational AI for trip planning and booking, offering live pricing updates. A spokesperson from Expedia confirmed that Layla would continue its operations and that its team would collaborate with Expedia Group's various brands, which include Expedia.com, Hotels.com, and Vrbo.

The acquisition is expected to be mutually beneficial, with Expedia gaining access to Layla's advanced AI technology and the expertise of its tech talent. This move is seen as a way to enhance customer experiences by providing more personalized and efficient travel planning options. According to Skift.com, regardless of Layla's future as an independent entity, Expedia is poised to benefit from the addition of tech talent to its workforce.

This acquisition underscores the growing trend of travel companies investing in AI technology to streamline and personalize the travel planning process. It remains to be seen how Expedia will integrate Layla's technology into its existing platforms, but the potential for a more sophisticated and user-friendly travel experience is evident.
