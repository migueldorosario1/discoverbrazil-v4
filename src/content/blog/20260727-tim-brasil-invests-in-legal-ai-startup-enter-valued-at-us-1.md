TIM, one of Brazil's major mobile carriers, has strategically invested in Enter, a São Paulo-based legal AI startup that has reached unicorn status with a valuation of US$1.2 billion (~R$6.1 billion) in May 2026, as reported by [riotimesonline.com](https://www.riotimesonline.com/tim-invests-in-enter-ai-legaltech-brazil/). This development not only highlights the growing importance of AI in the legal sector but also TIM's commitment to innovation through its 5G technology strategy.

**Enter's AI-Powered Legal Solutions**

Enter's sophisticated AI agents are designed to manage and defend corporate legal cases, focusing on consumer disputes and labor claims. By analyzing lawsuits, identifying fraudulent litigation, interpreting judicial decisions, and offering legal recommendations, Enter's platform is poised to revolutionize the way corporate legal defense is conducted.

**Funding and Valuation**

Enter secured its unicorn valuation after closing a funding round that exceeded US$100 million (~R$510 million), with the round led by Founders Fund and featuring participation from Sequoia Capital, Ribbit Capital, Kaszek, Atlantico, and ONEVC. This substantial capital injection underscores the global interest in Brazil's blossoming legal technology sector.

**TIM's 5G Innovation Fund**

TIM Brasil's investment in Enter is part of a broader commitment to a 5G innovation fund, which has pledged US$50 million (~R$255 million) over two years, with an initial US$15 million (~R$77 million) deployed in 2024. This fund, in partnership with Brazilian innovation platform Upload, targets startups that can leverage 5G connectivity and align with TIM's digital ecosystem, making Enter a perfect fit.

**Strategic Implications for TIM and the Legal Sector**

Brazil's judiciary processed over 80 million cases in 2025, creating a massive demand for AI tools that can reduce legal costs for large companies. TIM's investment in Enter not only addresses the company's own legal exposure but also positions it to offer AI-powered legal automation as a value-added service to its enterprise clients.

**Brazil's Legal Tech Market and Enter's Expansion**

With the new capital, Enter plans to extend its AI capabilities into tax litigation and regulatory compliance and is looking towards international markets, particularly Mexico and Colombia, where similar legal dynamics create demand. The investment from TIM, alongside other prominent funds, provides Enter with a strategic distribution channel through TIM's corporate client base.

**Conclusion**

TIM's investment in Enter signals a significant shift in how industries intersect, with AI redefining the boundaries between telecommunications and legal services. It also highlights the growing role of corporate venture capital from non-tech incumbents as a funding source in Brazil's tech ecosystem.
