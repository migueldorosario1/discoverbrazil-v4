World Travel & Tourism Council (WTTC) CEO, Gloria Guevara, has expressed solidarity with the families, communities, and businesses affected by the devastating wildfires in Spain and France, highlighting the need for strengthened crisis preparedness and destination resilience, according to eturbonews.com. The fires underscore the urgency of closer collaboration between governments and the private sector to protect communities, travelers, and the future of Travel & Tourism.

In a record-breaking annual convention attended by Maribel Rodriguez, Senior Vice President of WTTC, the council strengthened ties with Destinations International, representing over 800 destination organizations worldwide. This reinforces WTTC's commitment to working with industry partners to support destination management and tourism growth, aiming for a more resilient global sector.

Guevara also congratulated Saadia Zahidi on her historic appointment as Director General of the International Air Transport Association (IATA), marking a new era of female leadership in the sector and emphasizing the importance of aviation for the Travel & Tourism industry.

Additionally, WTTC and the International Congress and Convention Association (ICCA) are exploring a strategic partnership to support the global MICE industry. This partnership aims to foster a more connected, resilient, and sustainable global Travel & Tourism sector.

Avolta, a leader in travel retail and food & beverage, has joined WTTC as a Global Member. Operating across airports, motorways, cruise lines, ferries, and rail networks in over 70 countries, Avolta's partnership is a testament to the commitment to advancing the global Travel & Tourism sector.

WTTC congratulated Spain on winning the FIFA World Cup, highlighting the role of major sporting events in driving travel and boosting tourism, as well as providing economic and social benefits to host communities.

Furthermore, WTTC President & CEO Gloria Guevara Manzo met with India’s Union Minister of Tourism to discuss expanding India’s global tourism profile and accelerating inbound visitor growth. This collaboration aims to enhance India’s competitiveness as a global tourism destination.

In another initiative, WTTC launched a global campaign to identify the 7 Contemporary Wonders of the World since 1801. Aiming to celebrate modern achievements that have transformed destinations and generated economic and cultural benefits, the campaign encourages nominations from people worldwide.
