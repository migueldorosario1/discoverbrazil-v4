Rio de Janeiro pulses with an energy like no other during Carnival. The samba dancers, the lively music, and the electrifying crowd create an unforgettable experience. Locals and visitors come together to celebrate the world's most famous festival.

According to rio.com, the 2027 Rio Carnival will feature the top 12 samba schools in the Special Group parading at the Sambadrome on Carnival Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday — February 7th, 8th, and 9th. The Série Ouro Group, comprising 16 other samba schools, will compete on Friday and Saturday, February 5th and 6th, vying for a spot in next year's Special Group.

## The Sambadrome Experience

The Sambadrome is the stage for the main parades. Each samba school has up to 80 minutes to showcase its theme, music, costumes, and choreography. Themes range from politics to arts and sports, with schools competing to be the most creative. Past surprises include levitation acts and flying astronauts.

## How to Get There

The safest way to reach the Sambadrome is via Rio's subway system. Ticket packages for the parades can be purchased online. For any questions, a Hospitality Desk is available to assist.

## The Prelude: Technical Rehearsals

In the two weekends leading up to Carnival, samba schools hold technical rehearsals at the Sambadrome. These rehearsals are a preview of the extravagant spectacle that the world eagerly awaits.

## A Brief History

Carnival's origins trace back to ancient Roman pagan celebrations. In Rio, Carnival balls became popular in the 19th century, with legendary events at the Copacabana Palace Hotel and the Municipal Theater. The Afro-Brazilian influence of samba led to the first Samba Parade in the 1930s. Rio's ethnic diversity makes its Carnival unique.

## The Nights of Competition

The festival kicks off on Thursday with the opening ceremony, where the mayor crowns King Momo. The first samba school parade sets the tone for five days of revelry. Friday and Saturday feature the Série Ouro Group, while Sunday, Monday, and Tuesday showcase the elite schools competing for the championship.
