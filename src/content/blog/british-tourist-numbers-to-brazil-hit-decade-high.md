British tourist numbers to Brazil have reached a decade high, Travel Daily Media reports.
The milestone marks the strongest level of British visitation to Brazil in ten years.
The report attributes the surge to growing British interest in traveling to the South American country.
Brazil has drawn more British tourists than at any point over the past decade, per the outlet.
Travel Daily Media covers the travel industry and reported the development.
