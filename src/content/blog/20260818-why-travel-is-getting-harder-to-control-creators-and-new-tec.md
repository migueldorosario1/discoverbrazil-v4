The global travel industry is entering a phase where control is slipping away from traditional gatekeepers. According to a recent episode of the Skift podcast, two forces are driving this shift: the creator economy's growing influence on hotel brands and the findings of Skift Research's State of Travel 2026 report.

Sarah Kopit and Seth Borko, the hosts, unpack how independent reviewers and content creators are now shaping traveler decisions more than ever. The discussion highlights the Aman creator controversy, a case that shows how a single influencer's experience can ripple across a brand's reputation.

Hotels, the hosts argue, need a new playbook for dealing with this reality. The old approach of managing public relations through official channels is no longer enough. Instead, brands must engage authentically with a landscape where every guest can broadcast their stay to thousands.

The episode also introduces the Travel Stack framework, a model that breaks down how consumers, commerce, operations, and experiences are evolving across the industry. This framework helps make sense of the complex changes happening in travel, from booking to on-the-ground experiences.

For Brazil, these insights are particularly relevant. As the country continues to attract international visitors, understanding the power of creators and the need for agile operations becomes crucial. Destinations like Rio de Janeiro, the Amazon, and the beaches of the Northeast are already seeing the impact of influencer-driven tourism.

The State of Travel 2026 report offers a data-driven look at these trends. While the podcast does not dive into specific numbers, the overarching message is clear: the industry must adapt to a more fluid, less controllable environment.

For hoteliers and tourism operators in Brazil, the takeaway is to embrace transparency and build direct relationships with guests. Relying on outdated control mechanisms will only leave brands behind.

The creator economy is not a passing fad. It is a structural shift that demands new strategies. Whether it is responding to a viral review or leveraging user-generated content, the playbook is being rewritten.

Skift's analysis serves as a valuable resource for anyone looking to understand where travel is headed. The full episode is available on major podcast platforms, including Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and YouTube.

As Brazil positions itself as a world-class destination, staying ahead of these trends will be key. The future of travel belongs to those who can navigate its increasing complexity with flexibility and creativity.
